Snowfox's top tip

Let go of ZL when a big attack is coming

Locking on slows you down and stops you dodge-rolling. When you see an opponent wind up a big move such as Hydro Pump or Solar Beam, release ZL, run and roll, then lock back on to strike while their move is on cooldown.
