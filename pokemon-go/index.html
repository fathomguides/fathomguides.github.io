Snowfox's top tip

Rank the GO Pass before you grind

During Halloween 2026 the GO Pass milestones switch on the catch bonuses: Rank 10 for 2× Candy and Rank 20 for 2× Stardust (3× with Deluxe). Catches before the milestone only get the normal amount, so push the pass first.
