Top tip

Your first iron ingot goes into a shield

Not a sword, not a helmet. A shield costs six planks and one iron ingot, and it blocks arrows, most melee hits and even creeper blasts in front of you. On Bedrock you can raise it just by sneaking.

What it doesType this in chat
Switch game mode/gamemode creative or /gamemode survival

Turn on Allow Cheats (Java) or Activate Cheats (Bedrock) in your world settings first. On console, open chat with right on the D-pad. On Bedrock, turning cheats on permanently disables achievements for that world. Use commands at your own risk and back up your world first. See our disclaimer.
