Snowfox's top tip

Climb wide, not tall

Below 1,000 Trophies a win is worth about ten and losses are small. Above 2,000 every win is worth less and every loss costs more. Push ten Brawlers to 1,000 instead of one to 4,000: same total, far less pain.

How to redeem
  1. Reward links and QR codes from official Brawl Stars channels open the game and add the reward.
  2. Supercell Store: log in at store.supercell.com with your Supercell ID and use Enter code.
  3. In game: Shop, then the Resources section, then Enter Code.

Free Gem generators are scams. Never enter your Supercell ID code anywhere except the game and the official Supercell Store.
